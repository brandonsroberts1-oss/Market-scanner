This is analysis software, not advice. Conviction scores rank how well a setup matches historically-studied conditions; they are not predictions and they are wrong regularly. Short-dated options can lose their entire value in a single session. Expected values marked model assume the scanner's directional read is correct — which is exactly the thing being assumed. Backtested option P&L is modelled with Black-Scholes rather than replayed from historical chains. Paper trading fills cross part of the spread but cannot reproduce real queue position, partial fills, or a fast tape. Verify every price with your broker before trading.
